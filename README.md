## Executive Overview

**FSM License Center** is a professional software licensing administration and product-governance solution developed to support the controlled management of proprietary software products across their operational lifecycle.

The project was created to provide a more organized, reliable, and professional way to manage licensing-related administration, improve visibility over licensed software usage, strengthen operational control, and support the day-to-day management needs associated with distributing and maintaining private software solutions.

Rather than treating licensing as an isolated technical function, FSM License Center approaches it as part of a broader software-product management process. Its purpose is to give administrators a structured environment for managing licensing operations, maintaining clearer oversight, supporting authorized use, and improving consistency across product administration activities.

This repository is a **public professional showcase only**. It presents the purpose, scope, business value, and development experience represented by the project while intentionally excluding source code, security-sensitive implementation details, internal validation methods, proprietary workflows, and other information that could expose or weaken the protection model of the system.

## Business Context

Professional software products often require more than application development alone. Once software is distributed for practical use, there is also a need for consistent administration, controlled access, lifecycle oversight, customer support, operational traceability, and protection of the software product itself.

When licensing activities are handled through disconnected records, manual follow-up, or inconsistent administrative methods, it can become difficult to maintain a clear and reliable view of software usage and licensing status.

FSM License Center was developed to address this broader product-management need by providing a dedicated administrative solution that brings licensing-related operations into a more structured and manageable environment.

The project sits at the intersection of:

- **Software engineering**
- **Software product management**
- **Licensing administration**
- **Access governance**
- **Operational control**
- **Customer and product support**
- **Information security awareness**
- **Business process improvement**
